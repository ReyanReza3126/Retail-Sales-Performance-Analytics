# Retail Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyses the Global Superstore dataset to understand retail sales, profit, customer segments, product performance and regional business trends from 2011 to 2014.

The main objective is to identify business opportunities and provide data-driven recommendations.

## Objectives
- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify the best-performing product categories.
- Study customer segment behaviour.
- Analyse regional and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit.
- Provide business recommendations.

## Dataset Information
- Dataset: Global Superstore
- Total Records: 51,290
- Original Columns: 24
- Countries: 147
- Markets: 7
- Time Period: 2011–2014

## Key Findings
- Technology generated the highest category sales.
- Consumer was the largest customer segment by sales.
- Central region recorded the highest profit in SQL analysis.
- Standard Class was the leading shipping mode by sales.
- Higher discount levels showed negative aggregate profit in several discount groups.

## Business Recommendations
- Review high-discount transactions to protect profit margins.
- Focus on profitable products and categories.
- Identify regional growth opportunities.
- Monitor sales, profit and performance KPIs regularly.
